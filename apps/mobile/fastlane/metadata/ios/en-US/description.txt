TRMission is a railway strategy board game set on Taiwan's railways. Collect train cards, claim the routes between cities, and connect them into a network that completes the secret mission tickets in your hand — while everyone else at the table is racing for the same track.

HOW A TURN WORKS
Do exactly one thing: draw train cards, spend a matching set to claim a route, or take on new mission tickets. Tunnels can demand extra cards you didn't plan for, ferries need locomotives, and a station lets you borrow a neighbouring opponent's route when missions are scored. Once someone's trains run low the last round begins, and completed missions, your longest route and station bonuses settle it.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Online for 2–5 — create a room and share the code, or drop into a public one. Spectators welcome.
• Team mode — up to six players in two or three teams; partners share one network and score a combined longest route.
• Bots at four difficulties, from Easy to Hell, to fill any empty seat.
• Offline solo play — a full game against bots with no connection at all, saved between sessions. Made for the MRT, the HSR, or a flight.
• Three maps — Taiwan & Outlying Islands, the Greater Taipei Metro, and a community-authored Greater Taipei Rail Transit map built for team play.
• House rules — station borrowing, the second draw after a rainbow, unfinished-mission penalties and more, all switchable before the game starts.
• Learn it in five minutes — an interactive tutorial, plus a rules Encyclopedia you can open mid-game.
• Seasonal leaderboard with ratings and win records, and your full match history.
• Never miss a turn — a push notification when it's your move (quiet while you're already in the app), and a Live Activity that keeps the game on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.
• Built for iPad — a three-pane layout on large screens that keeps up in Split View and Stage Manager.
• Traditional Chinese and English throughout.
